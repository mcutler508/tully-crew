The story

Playing Philly since 2012

The Tullamore Crew has been playing Irish Songs and Tunes throughout Philadelphia since 2012. We have the equipment and talent to fill a venue or provide intimate cocktail or corporate entertainment.

We've put plenty of time picking set lists and songs that can appeal to any type of Celtic music fan.
