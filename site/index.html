The story

Born in the snug

The Tullamore Crew started the way the best bands do: a few friends, a few instruments, and a corner of the pub that nobody else wanted. One tune turned into a session, and the session turned into a band.

Now it's seven of us on bass, mandolin, guitars, drums, tin whistle and fiddle. We play the old songs loud and the slow ones soft, and we're not leaving until the last chorus has the whole room singing.
